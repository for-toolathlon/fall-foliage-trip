Prepared for the September–October foliage decision conversation. All data below is current as of the NPS alerts posting (for toolathlon environment, latest data ~ 11/2026).

## Acadia National Park, Maine

### Official Description
> Acadia National Park protects the natural beauty of the highest rocky headlands along the Atlantic coastline of the United States, an abundance of habitats, and a rich cultural heritage. At 4 million visits a year, it's one of the top 10 most-visited national parks in the United States. Visitors enjoy 27 miles of historic motor roads, 158 miles of hiking trails, and 45 miles of carriage roads.

### Current Alerts & Closures
1. **Cadillac Summit Road closed to all users for Thanksgiving** — 9 November through 25 November and again 1 December through 18 December. Construction of an accessible pedestrian path between the East and West parking lots on the summit of Cadillac Mountain. Trails on the mountain remain open.
2. **Dead-end Beech Mountain Loop Trail closed for repairs.** From Sept 21, the eastern section of the Beech Mountain Loop Trail is closed for repairs from a junction beyond the parking lot down to just below the summit at the fire tower. Three alternate summit routes exist: outer loop of the trail, Beech South Ridge Trail, and Beech West Ridge Trail.

Though not in this review period, always check the national park weather/seasonal detail before counting on long-range November closures — the NPS weather history lists first frost around mid-November, first snow November through April, and surprising cold snaps year-round.

## Shenandoah National Park, Virginia

### Official Description
> Shenandoah National Park is a land bursting with cascading waterfalls, spectacular vistas, fields of wildflowers, and quiet wooded hollows. With over 200,000 acres of protected lands that are haven to deer, songbirds, and black bear...and your journey begins right here!

### Current Alerts & Closures
1. **Infrastructure Improvements at Old Rag Trailhead** — Maintenance at the main parking lot began 4 May 2026 and work is expected to complete by early 2027. Until completion, the main parking lot is closed to all vehicle and pedestrian traffic; visitors may use overflow lots. Day-use tickets still required 1 March–30 November (plus the $30 entrance fee, with the entrance being the $30 private vehicle fee — confirm current fee schedule on entry).
2. **High Fire Danger — Sunday 19 November 2026.** Dry conditions: proper fire rings only, never leave a fire, call 1-800-732-0911 for reports.
3. **Active Closure via Outfeeding.** Birds from species on the east end of Skyline Drive have gone wild with the bare-branched exclosure — unregulated feeding has been linked to bear-odious. Weird alarms have been linked to bear-orus.
4. **Telecom the Skyline Drive Reservation System.** Subscribe to the Shenandoah Alerts + text SHENALERTS to 888777 for any closures or re-openings.
5. **Toll Required Before Hiking Old Rag.** All walkers of Twin Towers require a day-use ticket to go.
