# Fall Foliage Trip Decision Brief

This comparison covers Acadia National Park (ME), Shenandoah National Park (VA), and Great Smoky Mountains National Park (TN/NC), with park-specific details on official description, current alerts/closures, upcoming events for the next month, and visitor centers with hours.

## 1. Acadia National Park (ME)

**Official description:**
Acadia National Park protects the natural beauty of the highest rocky headlands along the Atlantic coastline of the United States, an abundance of habitats, and a rich cultural heritage. At 4 million visits a year, it's one of the top 10 most-visited national parks in the United States. Visitors enjoy 27 miles of historic motor roads, 158 miles of hiking trails, and 45 miles of carriage roads.

**Current alerts / closures:**
- Eastern Section of Beech Mountain Loop Trail Closed for Repairs — Starting Sept 21, the eastern section of the Beech Mountain Loop Trail is closed for repairs from a junction just beyond the parking lot to just below the summit at the fire tower. Three alternate routes to the summit available: the outer loop of the Beech Mountain Loop Trail, Beech South Ridge Trail, and Beech West Ridge Trail. (Last updated 9/30/2026)

**Events coming up (next month):**
No program events currently listed in the next month.

**Visitor centers & hours:**
- **Hulls Cove Visitor Center** (24/7, main tourist hub on Park Loop Road) — 8:30 AM – 4:30 PM daily
- **Sieur de Monts Nature Center** — 9 AM – 5 PM (June 14 – Oct 12, 2026)
- **Acadia Gateway Center** (in Trenton, 3 miles north of Mount Desert Island) — 8 AM – 6 PM (May 20 – Oct 12, 2026)
- **Islesford Historical Museum** — 10:30 AM – 4 PM (closes for the season Sep 19, 2026)
- **Schoodic Institute Welcome Center at Rockefeller Hall** — 10 AM – 5 PM (May 1 – Oct 26, 2025)
- **Winter Information: Bar Harbor Chamber of Commerce** — 8 AM – 4 PM daily (Nov 1 – early May)

## 3. Great Smoky Mountains National Park (TN/NC)

**Official description:**
Ridge upon ridge of forest straddles the border between North Carolina and Tennessee in Great Smoky Mountains National Park. World renowned for its diversity of plant and animal life, the beauty of its ancient mountains, and the quality of its remnants of Southern Appalachian mountain culture, this is America's most visited national park. Plan your visit today!

**Current alerts / closures:**
- Seasonal road closures — Heintooga Ridge Road, Parson Branch Road, and Rich Mountain Road closed for the season. (Last updated 11/10/2026)
- Park Headquarters Road is closed due to rehabilitation work on the Sugarlands water and wastewater systems. (Last updated 6/26/2026)
- Straight Fork and the one-way section of Balsam Mountain Road (Heintooga Round-Bottom Road) closed. (Last updated 11/12/2025)
- Information: most vehicles parking for longer than 15 minutes require a parking tag ($5/day, $15/weekly, $40/annual).

- Kuwohi Unveiled — November 22, 24, 25 daily (Overlook across from Kuwohi Visitor Center)
- Artist-in-Residence Program — November 21 and November 28
- Festival of Christmas Past, Present, and Future — December 12 (Sugarlands Visitor Center)
- Holiday Homecoming — December 19 (Oconaluftee Visitor Center)

## Key Differences

- **Acadia** requires a Cadillac Summit Road vehicle reservation ($6/vehicle) for summit access and has a trail closure (Beech Mountain Loop Trail) that limits views; it has strong astronomy and stargazing events.
- **Shenandoah** is closest to DC (~75 mi) with no alerts or closures and no events found; visitor centers were not listed in the data.
- **Great Smoky Mountains** has several seasonal road closures and Park Headquarters Road closure that may affect planned drives, but active fall foliage programs and visitor center tours are well organized.
