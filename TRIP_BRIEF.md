# Fall Foliage Trip Brief

Comparison of three fall foliage candidates: Acadia National Park (ME), Shenandoah National Park (VA), and Great Smoky Mountains National Park (TN/NC border).

---

## 1. Acadia National Park, Maine

**Official description:**
Acadia National Park protects the natural beauty of the highest rocky headlands along the Atlantic coastline of the United States, an abundance of habitats, and a rich cultural heritage. At 4 million visits a year, it's one of the top 10 most-visited national parks in the United States. Visitors enjoy 27 miles of historic motor roads, 158 miles of hiking trails, and 45 miles of carriage roads.

**Current alerts / closures:**

Three alternate routes to the summit are available: the outer loop of the Beech Mountain Loop Trail, Beech South Ridge Trail, and Beech West Ridge Trail. (Park Closure, last updated 9/30/2026)

## 3. Great Smoky Mountains National Park, TN/NC border

**Official description:**
Ridge upon ridge of forest straddles the border between North Carolina and Tennessee in Great Smoky Mountains National Park. World renowned for its diversity of plant and animal life, the beauty of its ancient mountains, and the quality of its remnants of Southern Appalachian mountain culture, this is America's most visited national park. Plan your visit today!

**Current alerts / closures:**

## Key differences

- **Best fall foliage color:** Acadia (known for mid-October foliage) and Shenandoah (Skyline Drive, Oct-Nov) are prime foliage destinations; Great Smoky Mountains' fall foliage runs Nov-Dec and is celebrated for early peak colors.
- **Weather/fees:** Acadia has entrance fees ($35 private vehicle) and timed entry for Cadillac Mountain; Shenandoah National Park has no listed entrance fees; Great Smoky Mountains requires parking tags ($5-40) for long stays and seasonal road closures.
- **Park accessibility:** Acadia offers visitor centers with clear seasonal hours; Shenandoah's visitor centers are not in the current data; Great Smoky Mountains has four visitor centers (Sugarlands, Cades Cove, Oconaluftee, Kuwohi) and seasonal closures that affect hikes.
- **Immediate closures to flag:** Acadia's Beech Mountain Loop closure; Great Smoky Mountains' seasonal road closures, HQ Road closure, and Balsam Mountain Road closure.
