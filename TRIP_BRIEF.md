## 1. Acadia National Park (Maine)

**Official description:**
Acadia National Park protects the natural beauty of the highest rocky headlands along the Atlantic coastline of the United States, with an abundance of habitats and a rich cultural heritage. It is one of the top ten most-visited national parks in the United States, attracting 4 million visits a year. Visitors enjoy 27 miles of historic motor roads (Schoodic Woods, Ocean Drive, Cadillac Mountain Road), 158 miles of hiking trails, and 45 miles of carriage roads.

**Current alerts / closures:**
- Eastern Section of Beech Mountain Loop Trail Closed for Repairs — Starting Sept 21, the eastern section of the Beech Mountain Loop Trail is closed for repairs from a junction just beyond the parking lot to just below the summit at the fire tower. Three alternate routes to the summit are available: the outer loop of the Beech Mountain Loop Trail, Beech South Ridge Trail, and Beech West Ridge Trail. (Last updated 9/30/2026)

**Events coming up (next month):**
- Drop-in Stewardship Volunteering with Friends of Acadia — every Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday, June through end of October; 8:15 a.m. – 12:15 p.m., weather permitting. Meet at the Hulls Cove Storage Facility near the Hulls Cove Visitor Center, Bar Harbor, ME 04609.
- Wetland Revival – Walking the Path of Restoration — a ranger-led 2.3 mile walk through the Great Meadow, outside Sieur de Monts Nature Center.
- EeeK of Ecology Halloween Festival for Children — Winter Harbor, ME; children 12 and under with an adult.
- Take Pride in Acadia Day — volunteers help rake carriage roads; ends with chili, corn bread, cider, and cake.

**Visitor centers & hours:**
- Hulls Cove Visitor Center — 8:30 AM – 4:30 PM daily (Park Loop Road, Bar Harbor).
- Sieur de Monts Nature Center — 9 AM – 5 PM (June 14 – Oct 12, 2026).
- Acadia Gateway Center (Trenton, 3 miles north of Mount Desert Island) — 8 AM – 6 PM (May 20 – Oct 12, 2026).
- Islesford Historical Museum — 10:30 AM – 4 PM (closes for the season Sep 23, 2026).
- Schoodic Institute Welcome Center at Rockefeller Hall — 10 AM – 5 PM (May 1 – Oct 26, 2025).
